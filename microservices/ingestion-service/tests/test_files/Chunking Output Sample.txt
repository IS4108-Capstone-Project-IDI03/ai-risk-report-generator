The purpose of this PRE report is to assist [PROPERTY] with the implementation of a Risk Management Strategy to prioritize risk improvements and mitigation strategies for their property, [PROPERTY] Development. It is made on request by Marsh Broking Office. The analysis carried out is based on data provided by a physical review of the location. The report will inform existing and/or prospective insurance markets of physical conditions at this location. This report is based upon conditions and practices observed and information made available to Marsh Risk Consulting at the time of our survey. The report should be used for risk management and insurance placement purposes only.
The visit comprised of an inspection of main area of the site including the electrical rooms, fire pump room, common areas etc.
Our visits will use National Fire Protection Association (NFPA), FM Global and local guidelines as technical references. Each site visit included the following activity:
- Physical tours of occupied spaces to observe storage practices, related property hazards and identify areas not in accordance with the above guidelines.
- A review of existing loss prevention programs, procedures, reports and documentation to verify status in accordance with corporate standards and practices.
- Interview relevant staff to gain a perspective on loss prevention programs and implementation to understand issues facing local management.
- An exit meeting with management to achieve agreement with our conclusions and to develop a recommended action plan.
This survey also included a review of administrative controls such as inspection, testing, and maintenance of fire protection systems and equipment; impairments to these systems; and fire emergency planning.
No physical tests were witnessed during the survey.

For the 35-storey office tower, the building is generally constructed with reinforced concrete structures (floor plate, beams, columns) with concrete flat roof. The exterior walls of the tower is made of full glazed glass curtain wall with thickness of 33.52mm and aluminum cladding. The surface of the glass curtain is coated with low emissivity coating silver and metal-oxide film, giving the building a blue colour. The interior walls are of masonry construction and internal partition walls in the office spaces are made of mainly drywall gypsum boards. The 47-storey hotel and residences tower is constructed of similar materials to the office tower. The building is generally constructed with reinforced concrete structures (floor plate, beams, columns) with concrete flat roof. The external walls are similarly glass curtain walls while the internal partitions are of masonry walls or drywall gypsum boards. The podium blocks and heritage blocks are generally all constructed of reinforced concrete structures with various exterior walls.
Sprinkler protection is provided throughout the offices, retail spaces and hotel with the exception of electrical rooms and risers. FM200 fire protection system installed at the server room on Level 3 of [SPACE] and Basement 1 of [PROPERTY] which are activated by cross zone smoke detectors. Gaseous fire protection system as also installed within tenants' office area within [PROPERTY]. Wet chemical suppression system is installed under the kitchen hoods within hotel kitchen and tenant's kitchen and maintained by hotel facility management team and tenants ' third party contractors respectively. Other means of fire protection include wet/dry risers, internal fire hosereels, portable fire extinguishers and manual break glass call points installed at strategic locations throughout the buildings as per local code requirements. Buildings are equipped with dedicated fire alarm systems which are monitored centrally at a 24/7-manned fire command centre, via a decentralized alarm monitoring (DECAM) stations which have direct connections to the local fire brigade.

Natural catastrophe for the sites are generally low, with the exception of lightning which is adequately addressed via the provision of lightning protection and earthing systems.
